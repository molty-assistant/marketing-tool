# Cold Outreach Templates — LaunchKit

**Purpose:** Convert indie makers into LaunchKit customers through personalised, value-first DMs.

**Principles:**
- Research the person first (check their app, recent posts)
- Be genuine, not spammy
- Keep it under 200 words
- Clear value in 1-2 sentences
- Soft CTA (no pressure)

---

## Template 1: Indie App/SaaS Founder

**Target:** Solo founders who've built an app or SaaS but struggle with messaging/copy.

**Context:** They've shipped something but it's not converting well or they're stuck on "how do I describe this?"

```
Hey [Name]!

Saw your post about [their app/launch] — congrats on shipping! 🚀

Quick observation: your product looks solid, but the messaging feels like it could use sharpening. You're solving [X pain] but that's not coming through clearly in the first 5 seconds.

I built LaunchKit to solve exactly this — paste your app URL, get a positioning brief + launch copy in 48h. It's like having a marketing strategist who's actually shipped products.

Would you be up for me generating a sample brief for [app name]? No strings, just to see if it resonates.

Cheers,
Tom
```

**Variations:**
- "Saw you on [social channel] talking about [their challenge]..."
- "Just checked out [app name] — love the [specific feature]..."

## Outreach Best Practices

**Before sending:**
1. Check their website/app — have specific examples ready
2. Scan their recent posts — find genuine conversation hooks
3. Verify they're a real indie founder, not a corporate account

**Dos:**
- Personalise every DM (their name, app name, specific observation)
- Lead with value, not "I have a product"
- Keep it conversational, not salesy
- Offer a sample/preview before asking for money

**Don'ts:**
- Copy-paste the same message to 50 people
- Use generic "hey there" openings
- Lead with "check out my product"
- Send DMs to people who've never posted about relevant challenges

**Platform-specific notes:**
- **X/Twitter:** 140-180 words max, check their bio for hooks
- **LinkedIn:** More formal, add context about why you're reaching out
- **Indie Hackers:** Join the conversation first, then DM

## Tracking & Measurement

**Track these metrics:**
- Response rate (goal: 10-15%)
- Sample brief requests (goal: 30-40% of responses)
- Conversion to paid (goal: 10-20% of samples)

**What works:**
- Specific feedback on their app
- Genuine conversation hooks
- Clear value in 1-2 sentences
- Soft CTA ("no strings")

**What doesn't work:**
- Generic "hey there" openers
- Pitching before understanding them
- Long DMs (>250 words)
- Hard CTAs ("buy now")

**Day 1:** Send initial DM (Template 1-5, based on context)

**Daily target:** 5-10 personalised DMs

## Product Snapshot

**Name:** Sakinly
**Category:** Muslim Mental Wellness / Mood Tracking
**Platform:** iOS
**Tagline (current):** Mood tracking meets spiritual well-being

**What it is:**
Sakinly is a mental wellness app designed exclusively for the Muslim community. It combines modern mood tracking with deeply rooted Islamic spiritual practices — Qur'an verses and Du'a (supplications) that align with your emotional state.

**Key Features:**
- Mood logging with streak tracking (7-day, 30-day milestones)
- Qur'an suggestions for moments of gratitude (Shukr)
- Du'a suggestions for moments of hardship (Sabr)
- Integrated Prayer Times feature (submitted to Apple, awaiting review)
- Local milestones trigger feature suggestions and in-app review prompts

**Founder Story:**
Kaddy Gassama is a Gambian immigrant and solo Muslim developer based in the United States. The app was built to solve a personal pain point: phones often add noise, but they can also bring peace.
